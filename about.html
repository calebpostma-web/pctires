About PC Tires

A Family-Run Tire Shop in Chatham-Kent

PC Tires is a family-run tire and wheel shop based in Pain Court, in the heart of Chatham-Kent, Ontario. We give you the best of both worlds: the selection and pricing of buying tires online, with the trust and convenience of having a real local shop install them and stand behind the work.

The short version: Buy your tires and wheels online at a fair price, book an installation slot right at checkout, and get them mounted, balanced, and installed locally by certified technicians for $25 per tire. No subcontractors, no runaround.

A Family Shop in Pain Court

PC Tires operates under Postma Contracting Inc., owned by the Postma family. The shop is run hands-on by the next generation of the family — the young crew shows up, works hard, and gets you back on the road. When you call, you reach a real local who will sort out your size and price quickly, not a call centre.

We are proud to serve our neighbours across Chatham-Kent. Being local means we are accountable to the people we serve — you know where to find us, and we intend to be here for the long haul.

What We Do

We sell new tires and wheels for cars, SUVs, light trucks, trailers, and farm equipment, sourced through our distributor network and shipped free to our shop. On the service side, our shop handles:

  • Tire installation — mount, balance, TPMS reset, and disposal of old tires included ($25 per tire)
  • Seasonal changeovers of pre-mounted sets
  • Tire rotation and TPMS relearn
  • Flat repair (proper internal patch), bead leak repair, and valve stem replacement
  • Wheel torque re-checks after installation

Full service pricing and booking is available on our installation page and through Book a Service.

Why Buy From Us

Online-only tire retailers leave you to find someone to install what you bought. Big chains make you do everything in person. We close that gap.

  • Certified technicians — experienced, trained professionals doing the work in our own shop, never a subcontracted third party.
  • All-in installation pricing — $25 per tire covers mount, balance, TPMS reset, and disposal. No surprise charges at the counter.
  • Book online, instantly — pick your date and time slot during checkout. No phone tag required.
  • Local accountability — a real Chatham-Kent shop you can call, visit, and hold to its word.
