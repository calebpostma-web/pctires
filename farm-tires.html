Why PC Tires

Ag Tires Without the Runaround

R-1, R-1W or R-4?

R-1 is standard ag traction, R-1W runs deeper tread for wet clay — a good fit around here — and R-4 is the tougher industrial tread for loader work. Not sure what came off? Read us the sidewall and we'll match it.

Bias or Radial

Bias is cheaper up front and fine for older equipment and light duty. Radials ride better, put more tread on the ground, and pay for themselves in fuel and traction on big-hour tractors. We stock both — your call.
