Frequently Asked Questions

How much does the Pilot Sport All Season 4 cost?

In-stock sizes run about $237-$743 a tire as of July 7, 2026 - common 17-18 inch sizes land in the $250-290 range, big staggered 20s and 21s climb from there. Install $25/tire.

Can I drive the Pilot Sport AS4 in winter?

It's an all-season, so it's legal - but a UHP compound is not what you want on ice at -15°C. Our honest advice: winter set from December to March, PS AS4 the rest of the year.
