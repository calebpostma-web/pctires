Cheap tires in Chatham-Kent, priced honestly

Most people who call us asking about tires are really asking one question: what's the cheapest I can get four tires on my car for? That's a fair question, and it deserves a straight number instead of a runaround.

So here it is. Tires start at $83 on their own, or $108 fitted — mounted, balanced, new valve stem and your old tire taken away. A full set of four fitted is $432, $488 once HST is on it. That's four tires, mounted, balanced, new valve stems, and your old tires hauled away. Bigger rims cost more, and every size we stock is priced on this page so you can find yours before you pick up the phone.

What's actually included

The number you see is the number you pay, plus tax. Nothing gets added at the counter:

  • Mounting and balancing — on the machine, done properly, not eyeballed.
  • New valve stems on every tire.
  • Old tire disposal — no separate recycling line on your bill.
  • No shop supply fee and no alloy wheel surcharge.
  • Free torque re-check after 100 km, because wheels settle.

It's worth checking that list against any other quote you get. A price that looks lower on the phone often isn't once the shop fee, the disposal charge and the alloy surcharge land on the invoice.

Common questions

How much is a set of four tires installed?
Tires start at $83 on their own, or $108 fitted. A set of four fitted is $432, about $488 with HST. Larger rim sizes cost more. Every size and price is listed above.
Do you have used tires?
Yes. We keep a stock of good used tires and sets, and they're listed above alongside everything else. Used stock turns over constantly — if you don't see your size, call 519-397-4686 and we'll tell you what's on the rack today.
Are there extra fees at the counter?
No. Mounting, balancing, valve stems and disposal are all in the price shown. There's no shop supply fee, no environmental fee added at the till, and no surcharge for alloy wheels. The only thing added is HST.
Can I buy just one or two tires?
Yes. We show sets of four because that's what most people need, but singles and pairs are no problem. Call and we'll price exactly what you need.
What if my size isn't listed?
Call 519-397-4686. This page shows the budget sizes we move regularly, but we can get almost any size, usually within a day or two.
How do I find my tire size?
It's moulded into the sidewall of the tires on your vehicle right now — a string like 205/55R16. There's a full walkthrough in our guide to finding your tire size, or just tell us what you drive when you call.
Where are you and when are you open?
7144 Grande River Line, Pain Court — about ten minutes from Chatham. Monday to Saturday, 8 to 6. Closed Sunday.
